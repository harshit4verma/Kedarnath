Kedarnath Dham (श्री केदारनाथ)

Kedarnath Temple is one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, perched at an altitude of 3,583 meters (11,755 ft) in the Garhwal Himalayas near the Mandakini River in Uttarakhand, India.

Towering majestically behind the temple is the snow-crested Kedar Dome (6,831 m) and Kedarnath Peak. Built of massive interlocking grey granite stone slabs, the temple has stood resilient against the elements for over a millennium.

Interactive Features:

  • Walk freely across the campus courtyard, climb the front stone stairs, and walk inside the Mandapa hall.
  • Look 360° at the genuine Himalayan mountain wall and snow couloirs.
  • Walk up to the brass temple bells (ghanti) and ring them.
  • Do Darshan of the real Kedarnath Nandi statue with authentic photos and 360° view.
