# Kedarnath Dham 3D & 360° Real VR Darshan

An interactive 3D WebGL and WebXR Virtual Pilgrimage to **Kedarnath Dham (श्री केदारनाथ)**, perched at an altitude of 3,583 meters in the Garhwal Himalayas.

## Features
- **3D Temple & Architecture**: Accurately proportioned Mandapa, Garbhagriha, carved Shikhara, Urushringas, and Kalash.
- **360° Real VR Darshan**: Equirectangular photographic immersion covering the Temple Courtyard, Temple Close-up, Sacred Bhim Shila, and Evening Aarti.
- **Interactive Audio**: Authentic Kedarnath Aarti chants and resonant bronze temple bells.
- **Atmosphere & Lighting**: Toggle between daylight, sunset alpenglow, and festive evening Aarti fairy lights.
- **WebXR & Gyro AR**: Native WebXR support for Meta Quest/Vision Pro VR headsets and smartphone gyroscope motion controls.
- **Drone Orbit & Walking Tour**: Free walk controls, jump to sacred hotspots, and 360° cinematic aerial drone orbit.

## Quick Start
1. Run `start-server.bat` (or PowerShell `./server.ps1`)
2. Open `http://localhost:8080/` in your browser.
